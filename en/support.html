Help & Contact

How do I sort photos and videos?

Swipe sideways to move between items; swipe up to mark one for deletion and continue. Marking does not delete it immediately. Tap Undo to reverse your last mark and return to that item.

How do videos play?

Use the speaker button to mute or unmute a video without changing the default sound setting. Tap the picture to pause or resume, or drag the timeline to seek. Hold the right side during playback for 2× speed; release to return to normal.

How do I zoom in and see dates?

The photo date and time appear at the top of the main screen. Double-tap a photo or video to open it, then pinch to zoom and drag to explore. It stays zoomed when you let go. Close at the top right; photos also close with another double-tap.

Why does a received photo have an unexpected date?

The app reads the date and time recorded in the system photo library, shown in your phone’s current time zone. If a received or imported photo has no original timestamp, Photos may use its save date; the real capture date cannot be recovered from that alone. This app does not guess or change photo dates. Dates refresh when you return after editing them in Photos.

How do I crop a photo?

Double-tap an ordinary still photo, then tap the crop icon next to Close. Drag the frame and tap Done to save and return to the main screen. Tap Undo there to restore the photo before this crop. Cropping Live Photos, animated images and other special formats is not currently supported.

How do I review marked items?

Tap the eye beside the trash to see all marked items. Open a preview and swipe between them. Tap a thumbnail’s undo icon to keep that item. When ready, use the trash at the bottom of the page to confirm deletion.

How do I add photos to albums?

Enable Album Mode in Settings. Drag the current photo down onto an album cover and release. Hold near either edge of the album strip to scroll, or release over empty space to cancel. A checkmark shows which albums contain the item; tap to remove it. One photo can belong to several albums, and removing it from an album keeps the original. Hold an album to reorder it or drag it to Rename or Delete Album. Deleting an album also keeps its photos.

How do I browse just one album?

Open the overview, tap the album name at the top, then choose All Albums or an album from Photos. Photos and videos appear together. Ordered browsing, shuffle and the thumbnail strip on the main screen are limited to that album. Switching albums keeps all your marked items.

Does deleting from an album delete the original?

Yes. Tapping the trash and confirming removes the item from the system photo library and every album it belongs to, not just the current album. The deletion preview includes items marked across all albums. To remove an item from an album while keeping it, use Album Mode instead.

Swiping & Deleting

After marking an item or adding it to an album, browsing continues in your last swipe direction. At the end of that direction, marking moves to the remaining items on the other side; adding to an album stays on the current item.

Why does iOS ask me to confirm?

iOS requires confirmation to protect your photos and videos; third-party apps cannot skip it. This app lets you choose everything first, then confirm the batch together.

Where do deleted items go?

They go to Recently Deleted in Photos, usually for 30 days. You can restore them or delete them permanently there.

Why can’t I see all my photos?

With limited access, tap Select More on the main screen. Or open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → this app and choose Full Access.

What if I cancel the iOS deletion prompt?

Nothing is deleted. You return to the app and can keep organizing or tap the trash again.
