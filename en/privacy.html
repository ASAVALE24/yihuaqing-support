Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

This app collects no personal data and never automatically uploads photos, videos, media details or usage history. When you choose to share, iOS passes your selected content to the app or recipient you choose. The developer does not receive it.

Photo Access

Photo access is used to display, filter, organize, favorite, delete and share the photos or videos you choose. If originals are stored only in iCloud, iOS may download them through its Photos service. This app does not receive your iCloud account details.

Progress Saved on Your Device

Your album filter, browsing mode, last swipe direction, current position and local identifiers of marked items are saved on this device so you can continue later. They never leave the device.

Deletion & Recovery

Photos and videos are deleted only after you tap the trash and confirm with iOS. Deleted items usually remain in Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days.

Third-Party Services

This app includes no advertising, analytics, sign-in or cross-app tracking services.

Sharing you initiate

Only when you tap Share and choose a recipient does the iOS share sheet pass the selected photo or video to that app or recipient. The file is prepared on your device and does not pass through a developer server. How the recipient handles it is governed by the selected service and its privacy policy; you can cancel from the system share sheet.

Visiting this website

Only when you visit this support website may its hosting and network security providers process network information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, browser information, and security cookies. The developer does not use this information for advertising, cross-site tracking, or profiling.

Contacting support by email

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message text, and any attachments you choose to include. This information is used only to reply and resolve your support request, and is kept only as long as needed to handle it.

Contact

For privacy questions, email us through Help & Contact. Policy updated September 16, 2026.
